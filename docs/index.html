IR Forward — install the firmware

Put the firmware on a new WEMOS S2 Mini straight from this page — no software to install. Works in Chrome or Edge on a PC or Mac (not Firefox, not Safari, not on a phone).

1. Get the S2 Mini ready

  1. Plug the S2 Mini into the PC with a USB-C data cable (some cables only charge). Do this off the PCB, or with the 230 V disconnected.
  2. Hold button 0, tap RST, let go of 0. The S2 Mini is now waiting for new firmware.

2. Click the board you are building

Sender

In the room with the remote. IR eye + red / green LEDs.

Use Chrome or Edge on a PC.

Receiver

At the DSTV decoder. IR LED(s) pointed at the decoder.

Use Chrome or Edge on a PC.

All-in-One

Sender and receiver on one board, when the remote and decoder are close together.

Use Chrome or Edge on a PC.

Pick the port in the pop-up (often ESP32-S2 or a COM port), then Install. Answer Erase with yes for a new board. It takes about a minute.

The S2 Mini cannot restart itself after flashing over USB. If the page says it cannot connect to the device after the install, that is normal — the firmware is written.

3. Start it

  1. Tap RST on the S2 Mini (or unplug and plug in again).
  2. Its setup WiFi appears: IR-FORWARD-TX-…, IR-FORWARD-RX-… or IR-FORWARD-IN1-…. Join it with a phone and follow First setup in the README.

Other way: download the file

Download the file for your board and flash it with the Espressif web flasher at Flash Address 0x0: sender.bin · receiver.bin · allinone.bin

Later updates go over WiFi — see the README.
